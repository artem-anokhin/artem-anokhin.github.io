# Sources for advice and knowledge cards
Use only these. Phrase findings carefully; no invented numbers.

## Sleep
- Van Dongen et al., 2003 — 6 h of sleep for 14 days steadily worsens attention; people barely notice it. https://Www.Gwern.net/doc/melatonin/2003-vandongen.pdf
- AASM/SRS — adults need ≥7 h. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4434546/
- National Sleep Foundation — young adults 7–9 h. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29073412/

## Caffeine
- Drake et al., 2013 — 400 mg caffeine even 6 h before bed cut sleep by about 1 h. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3805807
- FDA — up to 400 mg/day for healthy adults (~2–3 cups). https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much

## Food and water
- Breakfast review (45 studies, children/adolescents) — breakfast helps attention and memory the same morning; evidence moderate. https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/100495/
- Dehydration meta-analysis (Georgia Tech) — noticeable decline from ~2% body-mass loss, attention suffers most. https://www.news.gatech.edu/2018/07/16/we-get-parched-cognition-can-sputter-dehydration-study-says

## Activity, stress, rest, phone
- Acute exercise meta-analysis (79 studies) — small but consistent cognitive benefit. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22480735/
- Acute stress meta-analysis — impairs working memory and cognitive flexibility. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27371161/
- Micro-breaks meta-analysis — breaks ≤10 min raise vigor and reduce fatigue. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36044424/
- Smartphone presence (UT Austin, 2017) — phone on the desk lowered task performance; replication only partial. https://news.utexas.edu/2017/06/26/the-mere-presence-of-your-smartphone-reduces-brain-power · https://www.bps.org.uk/research-digest/revisiting-brain-drain-effect

## Measuring focus and habits
- PVT-B (Basner, 2011) — 3-min reaction test, lapse threshold 355 ms. https://www.med.upenn.edu/uep/assets/user-content/documents/Basner2011-ValidityandsensitivityofabriefPVT.pdf
- Lally, 2010 — habits form in a median of 66 days (18–254); one miss does not break it. https://core.ac.uk/download/70653523.pdf
- Implementation intentions meta-analysis (94 studies, d = 0.65) — "if–then" plans help reach goals. https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/server/api/core/bitstreams/eb48c8cf-7855-478e-90ec-1ea6d44f8252/content
- Limits of personal correlations — they are hypotheses, factors are confounded. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/digital-health/articles/10.3389/fdgth.2020.00003/full
